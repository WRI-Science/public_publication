How this panel was checked
  • No label from the scored place was used for training, for the neighbour pass, or for choosing the blend weight.
  • CAL FIRE inspections inside Riverside County are held out together with Riverside.
  • Intervals (on hover) resample 2 km cells or whole fires, not single roofs.
  • Labels are mostly county records, which go stale after re-roofing; CAL FIRE inspections are the cleanest check.
  • The photo CNN is held out by groups of places (fewer training places than the features model), so it is slightly understated.
