Found and right, place by place, with a balanced cut-off

A map needs one cut-off per material. Here the cut-off is set on the other places only, as it would have to be in a new county. Rare materials (a few percent of roofs) come with many false alarms, because a small error rate on the many other roofs adds up; the class mix of a new county is unknown and differs a lot between places.Per place and material, the threshold maximising weighted F1 on all other places' never-seen predictions is applied to the held-out place. Weighted to the real mix. One-vs-rest; a roof can pass two thresholds.
