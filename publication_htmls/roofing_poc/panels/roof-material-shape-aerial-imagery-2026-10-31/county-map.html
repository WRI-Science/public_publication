By Carlo Broderick, NCEAS, UC Santa Barbara · 2026-10-31 13:09 PDT

Santa Cruz County, mapped in 500 m squares

Every building in the county was scored by the model built only from public CAL FIRE labels, then averaged over 500 m squares (1,326 squares, 95,987 buildings). Where the county records have at least 20 roofs, the model's tile share follows the recorded one (rank correlation 0.61 over 527 squares). It calls 9% of roofs tile, against 7% in the records. The right panel shows lidar roof complexity.Squares with at least 10 buildings, at least half of them inside the 2020 county lidar survey (a stand-in for the county line). How this panel was checked
  • No single building is shown; squares with fewer than 10 buildings are dropped.
  • The material model never saw a Santa Cruz label, so every square is out-of-sample.
