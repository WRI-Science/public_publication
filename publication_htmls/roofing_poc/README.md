How well can free aerial photos (several years of NAIP) and satellite data (AlphaEarth, Sentinel-2) map roof material, building by building? Current best model, each place scored as a county the model never saw, in 6 places (Santa Cruz CA, CA fire areas, Riverside CA, Pasco FL, Cook IL, Dallas TX). Material is the target; roof shape and complexity are a bonus. With what it would take to build a national map: label sources and costs.
