How well can free aerial photos (several years of NAIP) and satellite data (AlphaEarth, Sentinel-2) map roof material, building by building? Current best model, each place scored as a county the model never saw, in 6 places (Santa Cruz CA, CA fire areas, Riverside CA, Pasco FL, Cook IL, Dallas TX). Material is the target; roof shape and complexity are a bonus. With what it would take to build a national map: label sources and costs. Live: <https://wri-science.github.io/public_publication/publication_htmls/roofing_poc/roof-material-aerial-photos-satellite-data-2026-10-31.html>

Could we map roof material for every building in the US? Current best model, scored the way a national map would face it: each place predicted by models that never saw a label from it. 56 places across the US, about 10 million labelled roofs. Material is the target; roof shape is a bonus. With the training data, how the model works, and what more labels would cost.
