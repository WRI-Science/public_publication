How to read this page

Hover over, tap or tab to any bar, dot, cell or tile for its exact numbers. Colours mean the same thing on every chart: vermillion is burned, blue is directly exposed (inside the fire perimeter or at its edge), light blue is indirectly exposed (outside it, within reach of embers).

"Burned" is Carlson's label, read from photos taken before and after each fire.
