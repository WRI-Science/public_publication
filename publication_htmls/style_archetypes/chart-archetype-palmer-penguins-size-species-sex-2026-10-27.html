Chart archetype: Palmer penguins, size by species and sex · 2026-10-27

Bill, flipper and body-mass measurements of 344 adult Adélie, Chinstrap and Gentoo penguins at three sites in the Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica, 2007–08 to 2009–10: differences by species and sex with bootstrap intervals, and why pooling the species misleads.

A trap

Bill length and depth: together they point the wrong way

Within every species, penguins with longer bills also have deeper bills (r = +0.39 to +0.65). Pool the species and it seems to reverse (r = -0.24), because Gentoo have long but shallow bills. Always compare within species.Simpson's paradox: the between-species association (Gentoo: long, shallow bills) dominates the pooled Pearson r and has the opposite sign to every within-species r.
