The question at the heart of the book

Should we halt the use of the ocean’s resources—or learn to use them better?

Humanity needs food, clean energy, transport, minerals, and ways to manage carbon. The ocean may help provide all five. A Sea of Wealth asks what each opportunity offers, what it risks, and how we can make choices that work for people and the planet.
