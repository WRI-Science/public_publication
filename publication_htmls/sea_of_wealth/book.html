About the book

A hopeful argument for a thriving blue planet.

As governments and companies rush toward the blue expanse covering two-thirds of our planet, Ben Halpern asks not simply whether we should use the ocean—but how.

Blending cutting-edge science with personal stories, Halpern travels the globe to investigate food, energy, transport, minerals, and carbon. He weighs their potential against their environmental risks and compares them with the choices we already make on land.

The result is a clear-eyed case for approaching the ocean not as an untouched wilderness or unlimited storehouse, but as a living system whose future depends on wiser decisions now.
